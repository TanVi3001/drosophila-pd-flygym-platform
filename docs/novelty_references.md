# Novelty audit references

Search and synthesis date: 2026-09-30. Primary papers and system documentation were checked for the specific capabilities attributed below. This is a targeted review, not a systematic review or exhaustive search. Categories indicate publication status as of the review date.

## PEER_REVIEWED

1. **Lazar, A. A.; Liu, T.; Turkcan, M. K.; Zhou, Y. (2021).** “Accelerating with FlyBrainLab the discovery of the functional logic of the Drosophila brain in the connectomic and synaptomic era.” *eLife* 10:e62362. [doi:10.7554/eLife.62362](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.62362). Included as the closest Drosophila-specific integrated data/anatomy/executable-circuit platform prior.
2. **Pospisil, D. A. et al. (2024).** “The fly connectome reveals a path to the effectome.” *Nature* 634, 201–209. [doi:10.1038/s41586-024-07982-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07982-0). Included as direct prior art for connectome-informed causal estimation, circuit discovery and perturbation prioritization.
3. **Shiu, P. K. et al. (2024).** “A Drosophila computational brain model reveals sensorimotor processing.” *Nature* 634, 210–219. [doi:10.1038/s41586-024-07763-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07763-9). Source computational brain model and benchmark context; not a Workbench paper.
4. **Dorkenwald, S. et al. (2024).** “Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain.” *Nature* 634, 124–138. [doi:10.1038/s41586-024-07558-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07558-y). Included as connectome/data/query ecosystem prior.
5. **Schlegel, P. et al. (2024).** “Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing of Drosophila.” *Nature* 634, 139–152. [doi:10.1038/s41586-024-07686-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07686-5). Included to distinguish curated annotation and biological mapping from Workbench's computation-level checks.
6. **Wang-Chen, T. et al. (2024).** “NeuroMechFly v2: simulating embodied sensorimotor control in adult Drosophila.” *Nature Methods* 21, 2353–2362. [doi:10.1038/s41592-024-02497-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02497-y). Adjacent embodied behavior simulator; relevant to prevent claims Workbench does not make.
7. **Antolík, J.; Davison, A. P. (2013).** “Integrated workflows for spiking neuronal network simulations.” *Frontiers in Neuroinformatics* 7:34. [doi:10.3389/fninf.2013.00034](https://doi.org/10.3389/fninf.2013.00034). Direct prior for neural experiment/stimulation specification, execution, data storage, analysis and visualization.
8. **Stevens, J.-L. R. et al. (2013).** “An automated and reproducible workflow for running and analyzing neural simulations using Lancet and IPython Notebook.” *Frontiers in Neuroinformatics* 7:44. [doi:10.3389/fninf.2013.00044](https://doi.org/10.3389/fninf.2013.00044). Neuroscience-specific reproducible simulation workflow prior.
9. **Givon, L. E.; Lazar, A. A. (2016).** “Neurokernel: An Open Source Platform for Emulating the Fruit Fly Brain.” *PLOS ONE* 11(1):e0146581. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0146581](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0146581). Drosophila neural emulation and modular integration prior.
10. **Huber, S. et al. (2020).** “AiiDA 1.0, a scalable computational infrastructure for automated reproducible workflows and data provenance.” *Scientific Data* 7:300. [doi:10.1038/s41597-020-00638-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-020-00638-4). Generic provenance/reproducible-workflow prior.
11. **Wratten, L.; Wilm, A.; Göke, J. (2021).** “Reproducible, scalable, and shareable analysis pipelines with bioinformatics workflow managers.” *Nature Methods* 18, 1161–1168. [doi:10.1038/s41592-021-01254-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-021-01254-9). Workflow-manager prior; cited to bound generic pipeline novelty.
12. **McDougal, R. A. et al. (2016).** “Reproducibility in Computational Neuroscience Models and Simulations.” *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering* 63(10), 2021–2035. [doi:10.1109/TBME.2016.2539602](https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2016.2539602). Reproducibility standards/context, not direct competing software.

## PREPRINT / SOFTWARE DOCUMENTATION

Included because its abstract describes modular virtual experiments, evidence-aware execution, abstention and traceability; not peer reviewed as of this review and focused on E. coli, yeast and human K562 rather than fly connectomics.

## Search categories covered

Drosophila connectome analysis/exploration; whole-brain and circuit simulation; embodied fly simulation; computational-neuroscience virtual-experiment workflows; reproducible workflows/provenance; connectome-derived circuit and perturbation prioritization; and emerging evidence-aware/agentic virtual experiments. LLM/agent work was included only where it materially overlaps in evidence-aware experiment orchestration (PRAXIS-VirtualCell); Fly Research Workbench itself is not characterized as an LLM/agent system by this audit.
