## Closest prior system

There is no single closest system across every dimension. **FlyBrainLab** is the closest *Drosophila-specific platform*; **Pospisil et al.** is the closest *scientific objective* for connectome-based candidate/circuit prioritization; **PRAXIS-VirtualCell** is the newest close *architecture-level analogue* for evidence-aware virtual experiments, but is an unreviewed preprint in another domain. Treating only FlyBrainLab as the competitor would miss a direct methods prior for prioritization and the broad virtual-experiment architecture overlap.

Evidence/capability constraints, explicit abstention, approval-bound study configuration, run provenance/QC, and a human-facing shortlist may form a useful combined contract. This review has not established that the exact combination is unprecedented, that every Workbench path enforces it, or that it improves decisions. Accordingly the contribution is a **possible system integration**, not a demonstrated new algorithm or biological result.

## Conservative assessment

**NOVELTY_RISK = HIGH** · **PAPER_NOVELTY_GO_WITH_REFRAME**. A narrow computational-workflow/software paper remains plausible if it leads with explicit support/approval/failure contracts and transparent evaluation, and if authors document the exact distinction from FlyBrainLab and the effectome work. A broad “first evidence-aware virtual experiment platform” claim is not defensible after PRAXIS-VirtualCell, even though that paper is recent and not peer reviewed. Current development evidence also does not show full Workbench improving over effect-only, and the benchmark gates have no variation; this is a limitation of *this evaluation*, not proof that the gates have no value elsewhere.
