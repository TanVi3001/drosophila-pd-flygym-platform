## Conservative novelty assessment

**NOVELTY_RISK = HIGH.** The repository shows a potentially useful integration of evidence/capability checks, explicit abstention, experiment contracts, provenance/QC and prioritization. However, generic workflow/provenance and reproducible neural simulation are established areas; the development ablation does not isolate a ranking benefit from the gates; second-operator and held-out evidence are pending; and a focused prior-art search for candidate prioritization/virtual screening has not been completed. Frame this as a practical methods/system contribution and test its value, not as a novel brain model or proven biological discovery engine.
